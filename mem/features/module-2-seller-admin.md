Purpose: Seller tools, AI automation, analytics, inventory, and admin controls.

Features:
1. **Seller Dashboard** — Product mgmt, inventory tracking, order mgmt, revenue dashboard, sales analytics.
2. **AI Product Description Generator** — Descriptions, SEO descriptions, highlights, bullet points.
3. **AI Marketing Content Generator** — Ad copy, Instagram captions, Facebook ads, marketing emails, promo campaigns.
4. **AI Customer Reply Generator** — Support replies, review responses, refund responses, thank-you replies, professional seller replies.
5. **Inventory Management** — Stock mgmt, alerts, product uploads, editing.
6. **Orders Management** — Tracking, shipping updates, returns, refunds.
7. **Seller Analytics** — Revenue, conversion, product performance, customer insights.
8. **Admin Panel** — User mgmt, seller mgmt, product moderation, AI monitoring, platform analytics.
9. **AI Usage Monitoring** — AI requests, API usage, costs, performance, generation history.
10. **Platform Security** — Role permissions, secure APIs, auth security, rate limiting, fraud prevention.
