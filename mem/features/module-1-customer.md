Purpose: AI-powered shopping experience for customers — personalized shopping, AI product discovery, recommendations, smart search, camera shopping, budget shopping, review intelligence.

Features:
1. **Authentication** — Login, Signup, Forgot Password, OTP Verification, Social Login, Session Management, Role-based access.
2. **Landing Page** — Hero, AI showcase, feature highlights, testimonials, AI animations, CTAs.
3. **Home Page** — Personalized recommendations, trending, AI-suggested, recently viewed, smart categories, AI insights.
4. **AI Product Recommendation System** — Analyzes user behavior, search history, wishlist, purchases, interests, product interactions. Sections: Recommended For You, Similar Products, Trending For You, Smart Suggestions.
5. **Smart AI Search** — Semantic, voice, AI autocomplete, personalized, natural language, smart filtering.
6. **Product Detail Page** — Images, AI summaries, reviews, ratings, recommendations, wishlist, add to cart, buy now.
7. **Review Analyzer & Summarizer** — Summarization, sentiment analysis, pros/cons extraction, fake review detection, AI trust score.
8. **Smart Camera Shopping** — Upload/scan images, detect items, find visually similar products. Image recognition + product matching + visual AI search.
9. **AI Budget Shopping Assistant** — User enters budget; AI returns best value products, budget bundles, smart savings, optimized suggestions.
10. **Cart & Checkout** — Smart cart, AI discount suggestions, secure checkout, coupons, payment integration.
11. **User Dashboard** — Orders, wishlist, AI recommendation history, settings, shopping analytics.
